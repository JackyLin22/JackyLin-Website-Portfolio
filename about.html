My Design Process

1. Research

Every project begins with understanding the people I'm designing for. I use interviews, contextual inquiry, observations, competitive analysis, and usability testing to understand user goals, pain points, and the context in which they complete their work. I focus on collecting evidence rather than making assumptions.

2. Define

After gathering research, I organize findings using affinity diagrams, journey maps, and other synthesis methods to identify recurring themes. This helps me clearly define the problem before exploring potential solutions.

3. Ideate

I brainstorm multiple solutions while balancing user needs, business goals, accessibility, and technical constraints. Rather than settling on the first idea, I explore different approaches before selecting the strongest direction.

4. Prototype

I create wireframes and interactive prototypes to communicate ideas, test navigation, and gather feedback before development. Prototyping allows me to quickly iterate while minimizing costly changes.

5. Test

I conduct usability testing to observe how users interact with designs. Watching real users complete tasks often reveals usability issues that cannot be predicted during design alone.

6. Iterate

I treat design as an ongoing process rather than a final deliverable. Each round of feedback informs improvements that make products easier to use and better aligned with user needs.
